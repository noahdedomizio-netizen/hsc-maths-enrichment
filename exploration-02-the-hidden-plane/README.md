## About This Exploration

How can two complicated-looking trigonometric vector equations be understood through simple geometric relationships?

Inspired by Question 16(c) of the 2026 HSC Mathematics Extension 2 examination, this exploration investigates the geometry hidden within three-dimensional vector equations.

By recognising that a great circle is formed by the intersection of a sphere and a plane through its centre, students can transform an apparently difficult trigonometric problem into a more accessible geometric one.

The investigation develops connections between **vector parametrisation, three-dimensional geometry and vector projection**, demonstrating how deeper mathematical understanding can support efficient HSC problem-solving.

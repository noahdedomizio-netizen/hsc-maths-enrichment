# Exploration 01 — When Boundaries Move

*From the Fundamental Theorem of Calculus to the Conservation of Area*

**Beyond the Syllabus | HSC Maths Enrichment**

Inspired by Question 32 of the 2026 NSW HSC Mathematics Advanced examination, this exploration investigates how moving integration boundaries affect accumulated area.

Beginning with the Fundamental Theorem of Calculus, the publication develops a geometric understanding of changing areas before exploring how two moving boundaries can preserve a constant enclosed area.
